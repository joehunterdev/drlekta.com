Mixing

Mixing is where every element finds its place. Drums that knock, low end that's heavy but controlled, and vocals that sit right in the pocket — clear, upfront and never fighting the beat. Levels, EQ, compression, space and movement are all shaped so the track hits the way you heard it in your head.

Mastering

Mastering is the final polish. It brings your mix up to competitive loudness for streaming, tightens the overall tone and makes sure it translates — on headphones, in the car, through a phone speaker or on a club system. Releasing an EP or album? Mastering keeps every track sounding like part of the same body of work.

FAQ

How much does mixing and mastering cost?

Every project is different, so every project is quoted individually. Send your details through the enquiry form — the number of tracks, the genre and a link to a rough mix — and you'll get a quote back.

What genres do you work with?

All genres are welcome — hip-hop, boom bap, grime, R&B, soul, rock, electronic and everything in between.

Can I book just mastering?

Yes. You can book mixing on its own, mastering on its own, or both together. If you're not sure what your track needs, choose “Not sure yet” on the form.

What files should I send?

For mixing, send individual stems as WAV files exported from the same start point, with effects on the master bus switched off. For mastering, send a single stereo WAV mix with a few dB of headroom. Full details are confirmed when you get your quote.
